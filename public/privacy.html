American High Schedule

Privacy and about · Updated 23 August 2026

This is a student project. It is not an official app of American High School or the Fremont Unified School District, and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with either. Bell times are transcribed from the school's published schedule and the school's public calendar, but they can change. For anything that matters — a final, a minimum day — check with the school.

The short version

Your classes, grades and assignments never leave your phone. Signing in stores your name and email address, and nothing else. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising of any kind, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

Stored only on your device

This information is saved in your browser's local storage. It is never uploaded, and nobody — including the developers — can see it:

  • Your class names, teachers and room numbers
  • Your grade level
  • Assignments and any grades you enter for the GPA calculator
  • Your notification preferences, theme, and any schedule changes you add

Stored on the server

Only if you sign in with Google:

  • Your Google account ID, email address, name, and profile picture link
  • A session record, so you stay signed in. The session token itself is stored only as a one-way hash, so a copy of the database cannot be used to sign in as you.

Only if you turn on notifications:

  • An anonymous push address for your device, provided by your browser
  • A list of times at which your phone wants to be woken — and nothing about what those alerts say. The wording of every notification is worked out on your phone from data that never left it. The server does not know your classes.

Never collected

  • When you open the app, how long you use it, or what you look at
  • Your location
  • Anything from your Google account beyond your name, email and picture
  • Advertising or analytics identifiers — there are no third-party trackers in this app at all

Who else is involved

  • Google handles sign-in. The app is told your name and email; your password is never seen by it.
  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its database.
  • Your browser's push service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) delivers notifications. It is told to wake your phone, not what to say.

Deleting everything

Open Settings and scroll to the bottom. Delete account removes your account from the server, signs you out on every device, and erases the data stored on your phone. It cannot be undone, and there is no copy kept.

Age

This app is made for high school students. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no more from anyone than what is listed above.

Questions, or something wrong?

If a bell time looks wrong, or you want your data removed and cannot reach the button, email wenaydev@gmail.com.
